How the matching works

    
Each of the 6 questions maps your answer to weighted signals across 6 major AI models: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Llama, and Mistral. The model with the highest cumulative compatibility score is your match. Same answers always produce the same result — no randomness, no hallucinations, fully reproducible.

    
The compatibility percentage is normalized from the raw score, so a 94% match means your answers aligned strongly with that model's documented strengths, design philosophy, and "personality" as established by the companies that built them.

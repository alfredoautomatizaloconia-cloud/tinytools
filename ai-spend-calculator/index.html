How the percentile works

    
Based on aggregate data from AI usage surveys, the median professional AI user spends around $30–50/month on subscriptions. Power users (top 25%) typically spend $80–150/month. Developers and builders who also consume API credits often reach $200–500/month. Anyone spending over $500/month on AI tooling is in rarified territory — roughly the top 2%.

    
These estimates are based on 2025–2026 pricing for the most popular tools. API costs vary wildly — some developers consume thousands per month on inference alone.

    
Which AI tools are worth the money?

    
ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro are the two highest-value subscriptions for most knowledge workers at $20/month each. Cursor and GitHub Copilot are the clear winners for developers — each saves hours weekly. Midjourney Standard ($30/month) is the best value for image generation if you use it regularly. ElevenLabs Creator ($22/month) is the clear voice AI choice for content creators. Runway is expensive for casual use but essential for video teams.

    
The AI subscription trap

    
The average person who takes this quiz subscribes to more AI tools than they actively use. Subscription fatigue is real: you signed up for that $10/month "game-changing" AI writing tool in January, used it twice, and forgot to cancel. Run through this list honestly — you might find $40–80/month you're paying for nothing.

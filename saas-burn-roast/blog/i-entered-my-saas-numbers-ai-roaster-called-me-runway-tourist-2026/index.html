The 4 patterns the roast catches that spreadsheets won't

1. The Runway Rounding Error

Most founders calculate runway as cash / monthly burn. Clean math. Wrong number. The roast catches something subtler: it asks whether your burn is trending flat, growing, or shrinking — because a $30k/month burn today on a growth trajectory becomes $45k in 90 days. Your real runway is shorter than your spreadsheet says, almost every time.

The AI's roast line for this: "You have 14 months of runway if you freeze hiring, stop paying yourself, and your burn stops growing. Spoiler: none of those things are happening."

2. The Vanity MRR Trap

MRR is only meaningful relative to churn. A $20k MRR with 8% monthly churn isn't growing — it's a leaky bucket you're frantically filling. The roast flags when your net revenue retention implies you're in replacement mode, not growth mode. It phrases this as: "Your MRR is a treadmill, not a rocket ship. You're spending $3k/month to stand still."

3. The "We're Pre-Revenue But Spending Like Series A" Flag

This one stings the most for early-stage founders. The tool detects when your absolute burn number is high relative to your MRR stage — i.e., you've hired for the company you want to be, not the company you are. The roast doesn't moralize. It just calculates: "At your current MRR-to-burn ratio, you need a 4x revenue increase before this machine pays for itself. That's not a plan, that's a wish."

4. The "Default Alive" Test

Paul Graham's "Default Alive" concept — whether your company would survive to profitability if growth stopped — gets a ruthless application here. The AI models it simply: if new MRR acquisition dropped to zero tomorrow, how many months until you're ramen profitable? If the answer is "never" or "not in this decade," that's the roast. Most founders have never done this calculation explicitly. Seeing it written plainly — "You are not default alive. You are default dependent on fundraising conditions that may not exist in 6 months." — is clarifying in the way cold water to the face is clarifying.

How it works

    
Enter your MRR, monthly cash burn, MoM growth rate, and headcount. The tool calculates your burn multiple, runway estimate, revenue per employee, and time-to-profitability using standard SaaS finance formulas — then generates a deterministic roast based on your specific numbers.


    
The roast uses the same inputs every time — so the same numbers always produce the same result. Nothing leaves your browser. No data is stored, no API calls, no surprises.


    
What is Burn Multiple?

    
Burn Multiple = Net Cash Burned / Net New ARR. David Sacks popularized this as the key SaaS capital efficiency metric. Under 1x is great. 1–1.5x is fine. Over 2x starts raising eyebrows at your board meeting. Over 4x and you're basically the "we're still pre-revenue on the revenue" joke.


    
What does my grade mean?

    
The grade combines burn multiple, runway, growth rate, and revenue-per-employee into a single capital efficiency score. S+ means you're annoyingly efficient. F means your investors are refreshing their portfolio page and sighing audibly.

5 Ways AI Art Creators Use This Tool

Portfolio Protection

Block scrapers from your Midjourney or Stable Diffusion gallery

Your gallery page is pure gold for AI training: labeled images, style keywords, aesthetic tags. Add Disallow: /gallery/ for GPTBot and Google-Extended and that data stops flowing into future model training runs — while Googlebot keeps indexing your pages for search visibility.

Prompt Sellers

Protect your prompt shop from being reverse-engineered

If you sell prompt packs on your own site (or link out to them from a landing page), the descriptions and previews are scraped to teach AI models exactly what high-quality prompts look like. Block /shop/, /prompts/, and /products/ for all training bots while leaving payment and checkout pages untouched.

Style Protection

Stop competitors from "learning" your unique aesthetic

You've developed a recognizable visual style through thousands of iterations. When you publish your work-in-progress shots, style breakdowns, or prompt-to-image comparisons, scrapers ingest the pattern. Block /process/, /blog/, and /tutorials/ for Bytespider and CCBot to keep your creative methodology private.

Commission Artists

Protect client work shown in your portfolio

Commission artists often display client-approved pieces publicly. Those images — even if AI-generated — represent real commercial relationships. Use path-specific Disallow rules to block training crawlers from your /commissions/ folder while keeping the showcase visible to human visitors and search engines.

Content Creators

Preserve your AI art tutorial and YouTube thumbnail assets

If you run a YouTube channel, newsletter, or Substack alongside your portfolio, you likely host thumbnails, cover art, and banner images on your own domain. These assets — often your best-performing creative work — are prime targets for image-adjacent scraping. A blanket Disallow: /assets/ for training agents keeps them yours.

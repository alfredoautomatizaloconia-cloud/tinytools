Why AI App Builders Need Disclosures

The EU AI Act's transparency obligations (Article 50) took full effect in 2025 and apply to any system that generates or manipulates text, images, audio, or video that a person might mistake for human-made content — regardless of whether you're a large enterprise or a solo builder who shipped an MVP over a weekend on Lovable.

If your app has a chatbot, generates copy, recommends products with AI, or creates any kind of output using a foundation model, you need a disclosure. The good news: the disclosure itself doesn't have to be complicated. A single badge and a one-line footer are usually enough for most indie apps.

AI Disclosure Isn't Optional Anymore — Especially for Automated Shows

The FTC updated its endorsement and testimonial guidelines in 2023 to explicitly cover AI-generated content, including AI-synthesized voices. The EU AI Act's transparency requirements for AI-generated audio took effect in 2024. Spotify quietly updated its podcast content policies to require disclosure when a host's voice is AI-cloned. Apple Podcasts followed with similar language in their submission guidelines.

If your show uses ElevenLabs to clone a host voice, an LLM to write episode scripts, or any AI model to generate or substantially edit content — and you haven't disclosed it — you're out of compliance across every major distribution channel simultaneously.

But a generic "AI was used in making this episode" isn't enough. The FTC requires disclosures to be clear, prominent, and specific about what was AI-generated. The EU AI Act requires different language for synthetic voice content than for AI-written text. Spotify distinguishes between "AI-assisted production" and "AI-generated host voice." Every placement — show description, episode notes, audio intro, show website — needs its own correctly formatted disclosure.

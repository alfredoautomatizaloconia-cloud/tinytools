5 Places Your AI Shopify Store Needs Disclosure Labels

AI-Generated Product Descriptions

Shopify Magic, Jasper, or any bulk AI writer produces your product copy. The FTC's updated Endorsement Guides (2023) and the EU AI Act both require a clear signal that the content was machine-authored — especially if it makes performance or benefit claims. Use the generator to create a small inline badge ("✦ Written with AI") that you can drop into your product template's Liquid code.

🤖

AI Customer Service Chatbot

Whether you're using Tidio AI, Gorgias AI, or a custom GPT-powered chat widget, EU AI Act Article 52 requires that any AI system interacting with humans must identify itself as non-human at the start of the interaction. The generator outputs a compliant chatbot greeting disclosure you can paste directly into your chatbot's welcome message configuration.

⭐

AI-Drafted Review Response Templates

Using Shopify's AI review reply tool or a third-party app to respond to customer reviews at scale? If those responses appear to be personal, human-written replies, you may be creating a misleading commercial practice under EU consumer protection law. A lightweight disclosure in your response template footer solves this instantly.

📧

AI-Written Marketing Emails (Klaviyo / Omnisend)

Klaviyo's AI email generator and similar tools let you produce entire email campaigns from a prompt. As AI-generated commercial email scales up, regulators are treating undisclosed AI authorship the same as deceptive advertising. The generator produces a ready-to-use email footer line ("This email was drafted with the assistance of AI") formatted for plain text and HTML email clients.

AI-Generated Product Photography & Lifestyle Images

Selling with AI-generated product visuals — whether from Midjourney, DALL·E, or Shopify's upcoming AI image tools? The EU AI Act explicitly covers synthetic images used in commerce. Get an HTML image overlay label and a C2PA-compatible JSON-LD metadata block to embed provenance information directly in your product gallery markup.

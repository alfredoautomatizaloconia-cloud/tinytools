What Is AI Text Roast?

    
AI Text Roast is a free tool that analyzes any piece of writing and tells you — with ruthless honesty — how much it sounds like it was generated by an AI like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. We look at sentence structure, vocabulary patterns, hedge phrases, transition words, and dozens of other signals that separate robot prose from human writing.


    
How Does the AI Detection Work?

    
We use a deterministic heuristic engine (no API calls, runs entirely in your browser) that scores text across multiple dimensions: lexical diversity, burstiness (variance in sentence length), filler phrases, hedge words, transition overuse, and the dreaded "delve into / it's important to note" combo that haunts every AI essay. The result is reproducible — the same text always gets the same score, which means your link is truly shareable.


    
Why Does AI Writing Sound Robotic?

    
AI language models like GPT-4 are trained to produce statistically "average" text. That means they avoid extremes: sentences are medium length, vocabulary is corporate-safe, transitions are overused, and everything sounds like it was written by a very confident intern who just read a lot of LinkedIn posts. The result is technically correct but weirdly smooth — no personality, no stumbles, no voice.


    
Common AI Writing Tells

    
The most common giveaways include: phrases like "it's worth noting", "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in", "furthermore", "in conclusion", and "delve". Also: perfect paragraph structure, zero typos, uniform sentence length, and the absence of any actual opinions or humor.


    
Can I Use This to Check My Own Writing?

    
Absolutely. Paste your LinkedIn posts, emails, blog articles, or cover letters to see how human you sound. Use the score to make your writing more personality-driven, less polished-to-the-point-of-boring. Your goal: below 40%. A bit of chaos is good.


    
Is This Tool Free?

    
Yes. No signup, no limits, no API keys needed. The analysis runs entirely in your browser using a deterministic rule engine. Your text never leaves your device.

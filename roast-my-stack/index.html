How it works

    
Describe your tech stack in plain text. The tool detects your frameworks, databases, infra tools, and languages, then scores them across four dimensions: Complexity (how many pieces), Hipster Index (how new/trendy), Legacy Risk (how old/deprecated), and Over-engineering Score (ratio of complexity to team size). Same stack = same roast every time, no AI API calls, nothing leaves your browser.


    
What the grade means

    
S+ is a lean, battle-tested stack that ships fast. A/B is solid with minor quirks. C/D is either legacy debt or premature optimization. F is "microservices for a todo app" energy. The grade isn't about which tools are objectively best — it's about fit between complexity and context.


    
The four scores

    
Complexity counts distinct tools/services. Hipster Index rewards or penalizes based on how trendy vs. battle-tested the stack is. Legacy Risk flags anything that peaked before 2015. Over-engineering penalizes complexity vs. team size — running Kubernetes with a 2-person team is a choice that will be made fun of here.

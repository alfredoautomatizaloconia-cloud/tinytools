The ChatGPT Power User's Anti-Detection Cheat Sheet

Based on the 9 heuristics, here's what actually moves the needle — not generic advice, but the specific prompt instructions that lower each score.

💡 Fix Em-Dash Density

Add to your ChatGPT prompt: "Never use em-dashes (—). Use commas, parentheses, or restructure the sentence instead." This single instruction eliminates the strongest AI signal in 2025–26 outputs.

💡 Fix Sentence-Length Variance

Tell ChatGPT: "Mix very short sentences (under 8 words) with longer ones. Don't let three consecutive sentences be the same length." Human writers do this naturally; LLMs need to be told.

💡 Eliminate AI-Tell Phrases

Prompt: "Never use: 'it's important to note,' 'in conclusion,' 'furthermore,' 'it is worth noting,' 'delve into,' 'navigate,' 'landscape,' or 'this is particularly relevant.'" These phrases appear in AI outputs at 10–40x human rates.

💡 Lower Perplexity Proxy

Ask ChatGPT for "a more casual, conversational tone" and "use uncommon word choices over safe, predictable vocabulary where it sounds natural." LLMs default to high-probability word choices; nudging toward surprising-but-correct words lowers this score.

💡 Reduce Filler-Phrase Density

After generating, do a manual pass and delete every sentence that could be removed without losing meaning. ChatGPT pads content to meet length targets; humans delete the padding. One editing pass cuts filler-phrase scores dramatically.

The 9 Heuristics — What Gets ChatGPT Outputs Flagged

These are the signals that commercial AI detectors — and increasingly human editors — have learned to recognize. Knowing what they are is half the fix.

Em-Dash Density

ChatGPT uses em-dashes (—) at rates 8–15x higher than average human writing. One of the most reliable 2026 AI signals.

Sentence-Length Variance

ChatGPT defaults to uniform medium sentences. Human writers naturally oscillate between very short and long sentences in the same paragraph.

Vocabulary Diversity (TTR)

Type-token ratio measures word repetition. AI recycles vocabulary in predictable cycles; humans tend to use more diverse word choices across a passage.

AI-Tell Phrases

"In conclusion," "It's important to note," "Delve into," "Navigate the landscape" — ChatGPT uses these at statistically anomalous rates vs. human writing.

Perplexity Proxy

LLMs choose high-probability, "safe" words. This metric estimates how predictable the vocabulary choices are — high scores suggest AI generation.

N-Gram Repetition

Repeated 3- and 4-word phrases within a single document. ChatGPT tends to repeat structural phrases, especially across longer outputs.

Transition-Phrase Density

Density of academic transitions like "furthermore," "additionally," "in summary." High counts suggest essay-writing training data patterns.

Punctuation Pattern Score

AI uses commas and colons in subtle patterns that differ from human writing. This score captures punctuation rhythm across the full passage.

Filler-Phrase Density

"It is worth noting that," "This is particularly relevant," "It cannot be overstated" — hedging fillers ChatGPT inserts far more than human writers do.

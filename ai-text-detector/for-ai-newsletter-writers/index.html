5 Ways AI Newsletter Writers Use This Detector

✉️

Pre-Send Quality Gate

Before every send, paste your full issue draft into the detector. Sections that score above 70% AI likelihood get flagged for manual rewriting. You've worked hard to build your subscriber list — don't let a single robotic issue train your readers to skim.

🔬

Isolate Which Sections Sound AI-Generated

Not the entire issue — usually just two or three paragraphs that you bulk-drafted with AI and never properly edited. The detector pinpoints exactly which sections break your voice so you can surgically fix them rather than rewriting everything from scratch.

🎙️

Train Your AI Prompts to Match Your Voice

Run AI-generated drafts through the detector, then rewrite the flagged bits in your own style. Compare the score before and after. Over time, this feedback loop tells you exactly which prompt structures produce output closest to your voice — and which ones always sound generic.

🤝

Vet Guest Contributor Submissions

You accept guest posts or partnership content. A high AI-likelihood score doesn't mean automatic rejection — but it does mean asking the contributor to add personal anecdotes, contrarian takes, or real data points. Your newsletter's reputation depends on authentic, specific content.

📊

A/B Test AI-Assisted vs. Fully Hand-Written Sections

Some newsletter operators track click-through rates by section. If your hand-written intros consistently outperform AI-drafted deep-dives, the detector's scores provide the data signal you need to decide where to invest your writing time versus AI delegation.

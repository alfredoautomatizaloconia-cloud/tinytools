The 5-minute SaaS brand decision

  1. Pick one vivid accent. Saturation 70%+, lightness either 25% (deep) or 65% (vivid). Skip the muddy middle.
  2. Drop it into the palette generator. Pick analogous mode for harmonious SaaS feel, or complementary for high-energy.
  3. Verify WCAG 4.5:1 contrast on accent vs background AND white text vs accent.
  4. Export as CSS variables. Paste into your :root or Tailwind config.
  5. Generate matching favicon and OG image. Ship.

What works for SaaS specifically

  • Cool palettes outperform warm for B2B SaaS in 2026 conversion data — trust over urgency. Common accents: indigo (#6366f1), purple (#a855f7), teal (#14b8a6), cyan (#06b6d4), blue (#3b82f6).
  • Dark mode by default on the marketing site signals "for developers" and converts better in technical buyer segments.
  • One accent, used everywhere beats five colors used inconsistently. Linear, Vercel, Stripe, and Anthropic all run on essentially one accent each.

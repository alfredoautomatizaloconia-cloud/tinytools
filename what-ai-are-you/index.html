How it works

    
Each answer increments one or more dimensions: directness (Grok), thoughtfulness (Claude), pragmatism (ChatGPT), exploration (Gemini), open-sourceness (Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek), citation-obsession (Perplexity). We score across 8 AI archetypes and the highest match wins. Same answers always produce the same result — no AI hallucination, fully deterministic, reproducible shares.


    
Why this exists

    
Because Buzzfeed quizzes built the early internet's traffic engine and the AI era deserves the same medicine. Also: knowing whether you're more Claude or more Grok is genuinely useful intel for picking a default chatbot.


    
The 8 AI archetypes

    
ChatGPT — the well-meaning generalist who always says yes. Claude — the polite philosophy major who'll talk you out of bad ideas. Gemini — the corporate intern with a sparkle filter. Grok — the cousin who got banned from Thanksgiving. Llama — the open-source crusader who'll lend you their tools. Perplexity — the journalist who cites everything. Mistral — the European one who's offended by your hyphens. DeepSeek — the quietly terrifying overachiever.

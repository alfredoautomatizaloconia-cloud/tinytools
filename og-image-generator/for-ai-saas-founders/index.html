How to Generate an OG Image in Under 2 Minutes

1

Enter your headline and subheadline

Use your product name + clearest value prop as the headline. The subheadline should answer the "so what?" in one sentence. No lorem ipsum — real copy converts better even in previews.

2

Set your brand colors

Paste your primary hex codes. The generator handles contrast and gradient logic automatically — you won't accidentally ship white-text-on-white-background. Use our Color Palette Generator if you need brand colors first.

3

Add your domain or logo

Include your domain name or logo mark so the image is self-identifying when shared out of context — critical for cold outreach and directory listings.

4

Export and deploy

Download your 1200×630 PNG and add it to your <meta property="og:image"> tag. Test your preview with a social debugger tool to confirm it renders correctly before launch.

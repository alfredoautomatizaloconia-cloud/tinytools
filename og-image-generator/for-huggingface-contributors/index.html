How to Generate OG Images for Your HuggingFace Releases

1

Open the OG Image Generator — no login required

Go to the tool, pick a dark or light template that matches your model's vibe. Technical researchers tend to prefer minimal dark themes; demos and Spaces often do better with vibrant, product-style previews.

2

Enter your model's key details

Title: your model name (e.g., "MistralMed-7B"). Subtitle: the task and key stat ("Medical QA · 89.3 MedQA"). Add your org name or HuggingFace username as the branding element.

3

Customize colors to match your HuggingFace org palette

If your organization has brand colors (common in company research labs), match the accent color to your logo. Personal contributors can pick any color — just stay consistent across releases to build recognition.

4

Download and add to your model card

Download the 1200×630 PNG. Upload it to your HuggingFace model repository under assets/. In your README.md YAML header, set thumbnail: assets/og-image.png. Every future share of that model link will render your custom preview.

5

Reuse the template for every release in your model family

Create a base template once, then swap the title, subtitle, and benchmark number for each model variant. If you're releasing base, instruct, and GGUF versions, generate three images in under 5 minutes total.

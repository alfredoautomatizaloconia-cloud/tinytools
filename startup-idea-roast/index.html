Why does my startup idea need a roast?

    
Most startup ideas die not from lack of effort but from lack of honest feedback. Friends say "great idea!" Investors ghost you. The market just ignores you. This tool gives you the brutal reality check you needed before you quit your job.


    
How the roast works

    
We run your idea through a deterministic scoring engine that checks for 20+ red flags and green flags found in startup ideas that succeeded vs. failed:

    
    
      
  • "Uber for X" / "Airbnb for X" framing (automatic -15 points)
    • 
      
  • Buzzword density: blockchain, AI, NFT, disrupting, TAM (each costs you)
    • 
      
  • Problem clarity: do you describe a real pain or a theoretical market?
    • 
      
  • Solution simplicity: can you explain it in one sentence?
    • 
      
  • Revenue model: do you actually mention how you'd make money?
    • 
      
  • Market size delusion: any TAM > $1B with no evidence is a red flag
    • 
    

    
The same idea always gets the same roast — no AI hallucinations, 100% deterministic, shareable and reproducible.


    
What investors actually look for

    
Real investors look for: a clear problem, a specific customer, a working prototype, early traction, and a defensible moat. "We're building a platform" is not a moat. "We have 200 paying customers" is.


    
Is this tool mean?

    
Yes. Intentionally. The startup world is full of encouraging noise. You need at least one voice that tells you when your emperor-grade idea has no clothes. Use this as a starting point, not a final verdict.

About Roast My Startup Name

Roast My Startup Name is a free AI tool that analyzes your startup or product name and delivers honest feedback on whether it's genius or doomed. No sales pitch—just raw, rule-based analysis on brand-ability, memorability, domain luck, and more.

What We Analyze

  • Brand-Ability — How well the name works as a brand. Is it professional? Does it age well?
  • Memorability — Can people remember it? Or will they forget it between conversations?
  • Pronunciation — Can it be said out loud without sounding stupid? Do people mispronounce it?
  • Uniqueness — Is it original or just another "Uber for X"?
  • Domain Luck — How likely is the .com available? What about other TLDs?

Why This Matters

Your startup name is your first impression. Bad names kill businesses before they start. Good names open doors. This tool gives you instant feedback so you can pivot before you've invested 6 months building on a terrible name.

Who Should Use This

  • Founders in naming mode
  • Teams deciding between name options
  • Product managers launching new products
  • Entrepreneurs checking the vibes of their name choice

Share Your Results

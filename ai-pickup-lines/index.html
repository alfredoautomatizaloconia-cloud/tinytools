How it works

    
Enter your name and your preferred AI tool. The generator uses a deterministic algorithm seeded by your name to select 5 pickup lines from a pool of 70+ original AI-themed lines. Your Charm Score (1–10) is computed from your name's character values — it's stable, repeatable, and entirely made up.

    
Everything runs in your browser. No data is sent anywhere. No LLM is used. The lines are pre-written by a human who has spent too much time around AI tools and not enough time around people.

    
Why AI pickup lines?

    
Because "Are you a transformer? Because you've re-encoded my attention" is objectively funnier than any normal pickup line, and the tech community deserves chaos. Also, sharing funny content about AI is one of the few ways to get engagement on social media without posting something depressing.

    
Use cases (completely legitimate)

    
Ice-breakers at tech conferences. Your Tinder bio. Your LinkedIn summary. Your conference talk opening. Your company Slack. Your next code review. Your README. Anywhere professionalism has already left the building.

    
The charm score

    
Your Charm Score is calculated from the ASCII values of your first name, normalized to a 1–10 scale. It is completely meaningless but also deeply accurate. High scores correlate with people who use the word "alignment" unironically in casual conversation.

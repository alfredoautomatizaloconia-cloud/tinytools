The Tech Bro Score Quiz

Silicon Valley has created its own distinct culture — a blend of hustle-culture evangelism, startup tribalism, founder worship, and a deeply held belief that technology alone can solve civilization's problems. But how far into this culture have you actually wandered? The Tech Bro Score quiz measures exactly that.

What Is a Tech Bro?

The term "tech bro" describes a specific archetype: someone who uses words like "disrupt" without irony, who wakes up at 5am for cold plunges, who considers their side project a "startup" from day one, who has strong opinions about which billionaire founder is "based," and who has read every Paul Graham essay at least twice. It's not a criticism — it's a taxonomy. Some people are proud of their score.

The 7 Tiers

Scores range from 0-100 across seven tiers: Normie (0-14), Tech Adjacent (15-29), Startup Curious (30-44), Disruptor Mindset (45-59), Serial Entrepreneur (60-74), Peak Silicon Valley (75-89), and the legendary YC Alum Energy tier (90-100). The questions cover LinkedIn behavior, Twitter/X follows, productivity optimization obsession, crypto opinions, founder worship, and daily routine habits.

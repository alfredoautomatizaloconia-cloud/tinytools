What we analyze

    
We fetch your page HTML and scan for 14 conversion signals: title clarity, hero headline, CTA presence and count, social proof (testimonials, reviews, user counts), pricing transparency, FAQ sections, trust indicators, video/demo presence, mobile optimization, navigation complexity, urgency copy, keyword stuffing in titles, and overall content density.

    
Every score is deterministic — the same URL always produces the same grade. No AI hallucination, no API calls, nothing personal leaves your browser except the page fetch request through a public CORS proxy.

    
Why landing pages get roasted

    
Most landing pages commit at least 3 of the following sins: a hero headline that describes features instead of outcomes, a CTA button that says "Submit" or "Click here", zero social proof above the fold, a nav with 8+ items confusing the visitor, or pricing buried 4 scrolls deep. Your visitors leave in 8 seconds. Now you know why.

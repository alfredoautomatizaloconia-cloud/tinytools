How Much CO₂ Does AI Actually Use?

    
Most people think AI is just software — weightless, effortless, clean. They're wrong. Training and running large language models consumes enormous amounts of electricity, most of which still comes from fossil fuels. Your daily ChatGPT habit, Midjourney sessions, and Copilot suggestions all have a measurable carbon cost.


    
The Numbers Behind the Calculator

    
Our estimates are based on published research and energy audits from AI labs:

    
    
      
  • GPT-4 query: ~0.001–0.01 kWh per query (10–100× more than Google search)
    • 
      
  • AI image generation: ~0.002–0.006 kWh per image (Stable Diffusion) to 0.015+ kWh (Midjourney, DALL-E 3)
    • 
      
  • Code completion: ~0.0001–0.0005 kWh per suggestion (smaller models, high volume)
    • 
      
  • Voice AI per minute: ~0.0003 kWh (inference only, not training)
    • 
      
  • AI-powered search: ~0.003 kWh per query (vs 0.0003 kWh for traditional search)
    • 
    

    
We apply the global average electricity carbon intensity of ~0.45 kg CO₂ per kWh (IEA, 2024) to convert kWh to CO₂ equivalents.


    
Why This Matters More Than Your Lightbulbs

    
A single GPT-4 query uses roughly 10× the energy of a Google search. Someone sending 100 AI queries per day generates roughly the same monthly footprint as leaving a 60W lightbulb on 24/7. At scale — billions of queries per day — AI data centers are a major and growing source of emissions. Microsoft, Google, and OpenAI have all disclosed growing energy and water usage tied to AI workloads.


    
How to Reduce Your AI Carbon Footprint

    
    
      
  • Use smaller models when you don't need frontier intelligence (Claude Haiku vs Opus, GPT-3.5 vs GPT-4)
    • 
      
  • Batch requests instead of sending many short queries
    • 
      
  • Use AI-generated images sparingly — they're among the most energy-intensive operations
    • 
      
  • Choose providers committed to renewable energy (Anthropic, Google DeepMind have strong commitments)
    • 
      
  • Offset via verified carbon credits (Gold Standard, Verra)
    • 
    


    
Frequently Asked Questions

    
Is this calculator accurate?

    
It's a best-estimate model based on publicly available research. Exact figures vary by model, data center location, and time of day. We use conservative mid-range estimates. Think of it as directionally accurate rather than precisely measured.


    
What about AI training emissions?

    
This calculator covers inference (running AI) only. Training a frontier model like GPT-4 reportedly used hundreds of GWh — a one-time cost spread across billions of queries. Including training amortization would slightly increase each query's footprint.


    
Does it matter where the data center is?

    
Yes, enormously. A server running on Norwegian hydropower has near-zero carbon intensity; one running on coal in parts of Asia has 10× the emissions. We use global averages, but your actual footprint depends on which region processes your request.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Lovable and Bolt apps ship with bad favicons?
Lovable, Bolt, and similar AI app builders generate placeholder favicons by default — usually a generic logo or a blank white square. These tools prioritize getting your app functional fast, not polished. The result: every app in your portfolio looks the same in browser tabs, and clients notice. A custom favicon takes 60 seconds and makes your work look intentional.
What favicon files do I actually need for an AI-built app?
At minimum: favicon.ico (16, 32, 48px), favicon-192.png and favicon-512.png for PWA installs, apple-touch-icon-180.png for iOS home screen saves, and a maskable 512x512 PNG for Android adaptive icons. If you're deploying a PWA (Lovable and Bolt often do this automatically), skip the maskable icon and your icon will get clipped on Android. Our generator outputs the full set.
Do I need Figma to make a favicon for my AI-built app?
No. Opening Figma for a 32x32 icon defeats the purpose of vibe-coding. Type a letter, emoji, or short description — the generator outputs a full production-ready favicon set in seconds. No design skills, no subscription, no export workflow.
How do I add the favicon to my Lovable app?
In Lovable, place favicon.ico and the PNG files in your /public directory. Add the link tags to your index.html head: <link rel='icon' href='/favicon.ico'> and <link rel='apple-touch-icon' href='/apple-touch-icon-180.png'>. Then paste the manifest.json entries into your site.webmanifest file. The whole process takes under 2 minutes — just tell Lovable's AI to do it and paste the file contents.
Can I use an emoji as my app's favicon?
Yes, and it's one of the fastest ways to give an AI-built app a distinct identity. Choose an emoji that matches your app's function — 🧮 for a calculator tool, 📊 for a dashboard, 🤖 for an AI assistant — and it will be instantly recognizable in browser tabs. The generator renders emojis cleanly at all required sizes.
